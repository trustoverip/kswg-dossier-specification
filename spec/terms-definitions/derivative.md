[[def: derivative, derivatives, derivative reference, derivative forms]]

~ A lightweight, short-lived object that references a dossier within a transactional protocol in place of transmitting the full container. A derivative MUST cryptographically bind to the dossier it references (minimally by including the dossier SAID under its signature) and derives all of its authority from that dossier. This specification recognizes two derivative forms: the [[ref: citation]] and the token.
