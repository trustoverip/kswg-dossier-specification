[[def: capture-time, capture time]]

~ The moment at which a served or packaged dossier stream was produced, and therefore the latest moment for which the stream can show key state and revocation status. For a verifier that fetches the stream, it is the time of retrieval. It is distinct from the [[ref: reference-time, referenceTime]], which is the moment as of which validity is judged, and from the time at which verification runs.
